Billing Enquiry and Adjustment Policy

Internal policy reference for authorised customer-support employees.

Use of this source

This fictional policy source supplies organisational rules and reference information for a learning activity. It does not provide an Agent’s task, role, output format or operating instructions. Those must be designed separately.

Policy purpose and scope

Purpose and scope

This policy sets requirements for investigating complex charges, recording disputed items and referring matters that require authorised billing action.

It applies when a customer cannot explain one or more charges, services or billing periods from the information available to the receiving employee.

Information to establish

Before selecting a pathway, establish the relevant facts from the customer, billing records and approved organisational information.

  • Each service and billing period
  • Recurring, promotional, usage-based and adjustment items
  • Information needed to investigate a usage-based charge
  • Previous case notes and whether a credit was approved
  • Conflicts or gaps in the supplied information

Policy rules

  • Separate charges by service, period and charge type.
  • Distinguish explained charges from disputed or unsupported charges.
  • Ask for information needed to investigate a usage-based charge.
  • Do not treat a proposed credit as approved.
  • Prepare a billing handover where a charge cannot be explained.

Records and referral information

  • Charge-by-charge summary
  • Items explained by approved guidance
  • Disputed or unsupported items
  • Important missing information
  • Recommended next step or billing handover
  • Actions requiring authorised billing review

Actions requiring separate authority

  • Declaring that a billing error occurred
  • Removing a charge
  • Approving a credit
  • Promising a refund
  • Modifying the account

Escalation rule

Refer the matter to authorised billing staff when a charge cannot be explained, information is incomplete or conflicting, the policy does not cover the situation, or the requested action requires authority outside the receiving employee’s role.
