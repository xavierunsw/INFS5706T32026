Customer Support and Vulnerability Policy

Internal policy reference for authorised customer-support employees.

Use of this source

This fictional policy source supplies organisational rules and reference information for a learning activity. It does not provide an Agent’s task, role, output format or operating instructions. Those must be designed separately.

Policy purpose and scope

Purpose and scope

This policy sets requirements for respectful support, information minimisation and specialist referral when a customer may need additional assistance.

It applies when a customer’s personal circumstances, service needs or expressed distress may require support beyond routine service handling.

Information to establish

Before selecting a pathway, establish only information necessary to understand the support request and its urgency.

  • The immediate service or communication need
  • The support request and preferred communication method
  • Only information necessary for referral
  • Whether loss of service could create significant impact
  • Whether prompt human support is needed

Policy rules

  • Acknowledge the concern respectfully.
  • Collect only information necessary to understand the support request.
  • Respect the customer’s preferred communication method.
  • Do not request medical details or proof that is not required.
  • Explain only support pathways described in approved organisational material.
  • Refer the matter to an authorised support specialist when required.

Records and referral information

  • Respectful case summary
  • Immediate need and communication preference
  • Information intentionally not required
  • Relevant support pathway
  • Recommended specialist referral
  • Decisions remaining with the employee or specialist

Actions requiring separate authority

  • Diagnosing or labelling vulnerability
  • Determining eligibility
  • Approving a support arrangement
  • Guaranteeing continued service
  • Requesting unnecessary sensitive information

Escalation rule

Refer the matter to an authorised specialist when the customer is distressed, the situation may involve significant vulnerability or harm, essential information cannot be obtained, or the requested action requires authority outside the receiving employee’s role.
