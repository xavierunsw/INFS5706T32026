Service Relocation Policy

Internal policy reference for authorised customer-support employees.

Use of this source

This fictional policy source supplies organisational rules and reference information for a learning activity. It does not represent Telstra policy or procedures. It does not provide an agent’s task, role, output format or operating instructions. Those must be designed separately.

Policy purpose and scope

Purpose and scope

This policy sets the information, timing and referral rules for moving an existing home internet service to a new address.

It applies when an authorised employee receives a request to move an existing home internet service to a new address.

Information to establish

Before selecting a pathway, establish the relevant facts from the customer, available records and approved organisational information.

  • Current service and account standing
  • New address and building type
  • Move date and the date service is needed
  • Whether the new address has an existing connection point
  • Availability check result for the new address
  • Previous relocation requests or case notes
  • Business or other impact if service is not ready

Policy rules

  • Standard relocations require at least 15 business days’ notice.
  • A connection date may be discussed only after the availability check confirms the new address can be serviced.
  • Where the availability result is unknown, refer the request for a manual address check before any date is discussed.
  • Temporary connectivity, such as a mobile hotspot, is handled by the relocations team and is not covered by this policy.
  • Business-use deadlines are not covered by standard relocation timeframes and require specialist review.

Recordkeeping requirement

Maintain a record of the move details, availability result, notice period, unresolved information, conflicts, customer impact and the pathway selected. Follow the organisation’s standard recordkeeping requirements.

Actions requiring separate authority

  • Quote or promise a connection date
  • Confirm that the new address can be serviced
  • Create or submit a relocation order
  • Offer a hotspot or any other temporary service
  • Waive or promise to waive any fee

Escalation rule

Refer the matter to the relocations team when the availability result is unknown, the required notice period cannot be met, the customer requests temporary connectivity, or the request involves a business-use deadline.
